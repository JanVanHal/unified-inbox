Unified Inbox

One Gmail inbox and one calendar for every account you add. Mail stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server of ours.

Paste the OAuth client ID from Google Cloud. The steps are written out in SETUP.md. You can change the ID later in Settings.

Google shows an “unverified app” warning while the client is in Testing. That is expected. Tokens last about an hour, then refresh on their own.
